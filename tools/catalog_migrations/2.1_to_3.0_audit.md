## Policy

Approved state-based reclassification: asserted failures stay defect; visible soiling, wear, fading, overgrowth, surface rust, and deferred upkeep are degradation; presentation-only or dated/basic opportunities are modernization. Paving: cracking/surface wear/minor settlement = degradation, heaving/trip edges/crumbling = defect. Water stains = defect (moisture evidence); cosmetic staining = degradation. Dents without stated functional impact = degradation (mix-022).

## Splits

### damaged_soffit_or_porch_ceiling (defect, deprecated)

Conflated failure (sagging/loose/missing panels) with finish deterioration (patchy/weathered) — different evidence and remediation.

- `soffit_or_porch_ceiling_failed` — defect
- `soffit_or_porch_ceiling_weathered` — degradation

### worn_or_stained_vinyl_linoleum (defect, deprecated)

Conflated damage (torn/worn through/lifting) with cosmetic wear (stains/discoloration) — a torn floor and a stained floor are different claims.

- `vinyl_linoleum_torn_or_lifted` — defect
- `vinyl_linoleum_worn_or_stained` — degradation

### scratched_or_damaged_flooring (defect, deprecated)

Conflated damage (chips/warping/broken tiles) with finish wear (scratches/scuffs) — hard-flooring damage and worn finish differ in kind and remediation.

- `hard_flooring_broken_or_warped` — defect
- `hard_flooring_scratched_or_worn` — degradation

### damaged_or_aged_roof_shingles (defect, deprecated)

Conflated failed weather protection (missing/curling/torn) with aging (worn granules, moss, discoloration) — an aged-but-sealed roof and an open roof are different claims.

- `roof_shingles_missing_or_curling` — defect
- `roof_shingles_aged_or_worn` — degradation

### outdated_or_damaged_cabinets (defect, deprecated)

Conflated damage (broken doors/drawers, water staining), finish wear, and dated style — three kinds in one id; a photo of missing doors and a photo of oak cabinets resolved to the same item and price.

- `cabinets_damaged_or_water_stained` — defect
- `cabinets_worn_finish` — degradation
- `cabinets_dated_style` — modernization

### appliance_damage_or_missing (defect, deprecated)

Conflated missing/broken appliances with aged/neglected and dated/basic ones — failure, deterioration, and style are three claims.

- `appliances_damaged_or_missing` — defect
- `appliances_worn_or_neglected` — degradation
- `appliances_dated_or_basic` — modernization

### plumbing_fixture_leaking_stained (defect, deprecated)

Conflated active leaks with staining/mineral buildup — a leak is failure (rule: asserted failure); staining and buildup without a leak are deterioration.

- `plumbing_fixture_leaking` — defect
- `plumbing_fixture_stained_or_corroded` — degradation

### stained_or_damaged_bath_fixtures (defect, deprecated)

Conflated damage (chipped enamel) with staining/wear — a chipped tub and a stained tub are different claims and remediations.

- `bath_fixtures_chipped_or_damaged` — defect
- `bath_fixtures_stained_or_worn` — degradation

### driveway_or_walkway_cracking (defect, deprecated)

Conflated the paving rule's two sides: heaving/trip edges/crumbling are defects; cracking, spalling, and minor settlement are degradation (Steven's def-009 ruling).

- `paving_heaved_or_trip_hazard` — defect
- `paving_cracked_or_settled` — degradation

### landscape_improvement_needed (upgrade, deprecated)

Conflated deferred maintenance (overgrown/tired yard) with discretionary improvement (sparse/plain landscaping) — degradation vs modernization.

- `landscaping_overgrown_or_neglected` — degradation
- `landscaping_enhancement_opportunity` — modernization

### ceiling_fan_mismatched_blades (defect, deprecated)

Conflated damaged blades (failure) with merely mismatched blades (visual) — a damaged blade needs repair; mismatched is presentation.

- `ceiling_fan_blades_damaged` — defect
- `ceiling_fan_blades_mismatched` — modernization

### outdated_or_damaged_vanity (defect, deprecated)

Conflated damage (water-damaged base, broken hinges), worn finish, and dated styling — three kinds in one id.

- `vanity_damaged_or_water_stained` — defect
